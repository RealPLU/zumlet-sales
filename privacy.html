This website

This is a static sales page. It sets no cookies of its own, runs no analytics, and has no tracking pixels or advertising scripts. If your browser requests a page, the hosting provider processes standard server logs (IP address, user agent, timestamp) for delivery and abuse prevention.

When you buy

Checkout, payment, invoicing and the delivery of your download are handled by Digistore24, which acts as the reseller of this product. Digistore24 collects the information needed to process your order — name, email address, billing country and payment details — under its own privacy policy, which is shown on the order form before you pay. I do not receive or store your card details.

What I receive from Digistore24 is the order record needed to deliver the product, provide support and honour the return policy: your name, email address, product purchased and order date.

When you contact me

Support runs through my community profile, communi.com/@warrenh, or through the contact option in your Digistore24 customer portal. Messages you send are used to answer you and keep a record of the issue. They are not added to a marketing list and you are not subscribed to anything by contacting me.

What I don't do

  • I don't sell, rent or share your information with third parties for marketing.
  • I don't email you about anything other than the product you bought, unless you ask.
  • I don't combine your order data with any other source.

Your requests

To ask what data is held about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted, message @warrenh. Order records that Digistore24 must keep for tax and accounting purposes are held by Digistore24 and governed by their policy; I'll tell you what I can and can't remove.

Digistore24's own privacy policy is the authoritative statement for anything that happens on the order form.
