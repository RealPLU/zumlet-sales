FAQ

Questions people actually ask

Do I have to use all eleven?

No. Most people start with three or four — Cross-Post, The Hook Engine, one growth module and Batch & Go OS — and add the rest when a specific bottleneck shows up.

Are these prompts or finished products?

Both. Every module includes a tested prompt chain and the templates, SOPs and checklists you need to use the output. You don't need to know anything about prompt engineering.

Which platforms are covered?

Cross-Post covers X/Twitter, LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok and Reels, and Facebook. The Hook Engine adds Pinterest, YouTube and email. Longform is YouTube-specific.

Do the modules work together?

That's the design. Cross-Post output feeds Remix. Voice trains every module to sound like you. The Hook Engine plugs into anything that needs a hook. Batch & Go OS schedules the session.
